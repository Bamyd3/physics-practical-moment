Precautions (WAEC standard)

  1. The centre of gravity of the metre rule was first determined by balancing it freely on the knife-edge before any readings were taken, so that the weight of the rule would produce no moment about the pivot.
  2. Parallax error was avoided by reading the positions on the scale with the eye placed vertically above the mark, and all readings were taken to the nearest 0.1 cm.
  3. The knife-edge was placed exactly at the centre of gravity of the rule so that the rule remained horizontal on its own without any additional adjustment.
  4. The masses were added gently and the rule was allowed to settle before each balancing position was read, in order to avoid oscillation.
  5. The experiment was repeated for several values of M₂ so that the average values could be used to minimise random errors.
